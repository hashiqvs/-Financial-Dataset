#  Financial Performance & Profitability Dashboard

##  Overview
The **Financial Performance & Profitability Dashboard** provides interactive visual insights into sales performance, profitability, manufacturing pricing, and product-level analytics across different countries and time periods. This dashboard helps stakeholders monitor key financial metrics and identify trends that support strategic decision-making.

---

##  Objectives
- Track overall sales, profit, cost of goods sold (COGS), and discounts.
- Analyze performance trends over time.
- Compare profitability across countries and products.
- Monitor manufacturing price metrics.
- Identify seasonal and regional sales patterns.

---

##  Dashboard Features

###  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard highlights high-level financial metrics:

- **Total Sales:** 118.73M  
- **Total Profit:** 16.89M  
- **Total COGS:** 101.83M  
- **Total Discounts:** 9.21M  

These KPIs provide a quick summary of business performance.

---

###  Country Filter
Users can filter all visuals dynamically by selecting:
- Canada
- France
- Germany
- Mexico
- United States of America

---

###  Profit Trend Analysis
Shows yearly profit performance allowing users to:
- Monitor growth patterns
- Identify peak performance years
- Compare annual profitability

---

###  Sales & Units Trend
Tracks:
- Sales variations across months
- Units sold over time
- Seasonal demand patterns

---

###  Product Performance Analysis
Provides insights into profit distribution across products:
- Amarilla
- Carretera
- Montana
- Paseo
- Velo
- VTT

Helps identify top-performing and underperforming products.

---

###  Geographic Performance Map
Displays profit and sales distribution geographically, enabling:
- Regional comparison
- Market performance tracking
- Expansion opportunity identification

---

###  Manufacturing Price Indicator
Shows average manufacturing price trends to:
- Monitor production efficiency
- Evaluate pricing strategies

---

###  Waterfall Analysis – Units Sold
Breaks down contribution to total units sold by:
- Countries
- Monthly changes
- Increase vs decrease performance impact
